A new company, built on years in the trade.

Malmequer bem me quer was founded in Guimarães, in the Ave Valley, by Jorge Mendes. The company is recent; the experience is not. Many years of work in the sector came first, and setting up our own production was the step that was missing.

We are a family business: the founder's mother and sister work here too. We make women's briefs, all manufactured in Portugal, with no intermediaries between production and your shop. When you talk to us, you talk to the people who make them.

We work with shops and resellers in Portugal and Spain, and consider other markets on request. Terms are set case by case, according to each customer's profile and volume. What we agree, we deliver.

The name comes from the Portuguese daisy game: loves me, loves me not.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?

Minimum orders and resale terms are set case by case, according to the shop's profile and expected volume. Message us on WhatsApp for a proposal.

Which sizes are available?

Availability by style and size is confirmed when you contact us. Tell us which references interest you and we will send the details for each one.

How long does delivery take?

Lead time depends on destination and order quantity, and is confirmed before the order is finalised.

Do you ship outside Portugal and Spain?

Other countries are considered on request. Tell us the destination and expected volume, and we will confirm the shipping options.

Who is the catalogue for?

Shops and resellers. This site does not sell to the public: it is there to present the range and let you ask for resale terms.

Can I get the composition of each style?

Yes. Composition and sizes are provided on request, reference by reference.

How does payment work?

Payment terms are agreed directly with us before the order is finalised, with no middlemen.

Ask for our resale terms.

Tell us which references interest you, where your shop is and what kind of business you run. We reply in Portuguese, Spanish or English, with a proposal suited to your case.
